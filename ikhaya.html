About Ikhaya Golden Mic

Ikhaya Golden Mic is a flagship youth talent development programme under the Nomduva Community Empowerment Project (NPO Registration: 308-012). We provide a structured platform for aspiring young artists to showcase their performing talents, receive professional mentorship, build confidence, and steer away from substance misuse through engagement in positive creative activities.

1. Auditions & Discovery

Open talent search across local communities to identify passionate upcoming vocalists, rappers, actors, and spoken word artists.

2. Mentorship & Workshops

Participants undergo rigorous workshops covering stage presence, music composition, scriptwriting, voice modulation, and career discipline.

3. Showcase Finale

A high-energy grand stage event bringing together community members, industry leaders, and sponsors to celebrate emerging creative talent.

Performance Categories

Explore the core creative pillars featured in the Ikhaya Golden Mic initiative.

Vocal Performance

Solo vocalists across Afro-pop, R&B, Gospel, and local contemporary genres.

Rap & Hip-Hop

Lyrical expression highlighting community stories, conscious messaging, and youth experiences.

Acting & Drama

Stage performance, theatrical dialogue, screen presence, and character development.

Poetry & Spoken Word

Powerful spoken word expression sharing stories of resilience, hope, and social awareness.
